# Uplift Path — Homepage QA Report

**Date:** 2026-08-26
**Scope:** `/` (homepage) only, plus the shared navbar and footer as they render on `/`
**Breakpoints:** desktop 1440×900 and mobile 375×812 (reloaded after each resize)
**Method:** live walk of the rendered page at `http://localhost:3000`, computed-style and
geometry sampling, link/button/form audit, console + network capture.
**Note:** the browser pane in this environment could not composite frames, so screenshots were
unavailable. All visual findings below were established from rendered geometry, computed styles
and measured contrast rather than from images — every claim is backed by a measured value.

**Baseline for "what should be here":** `.claude/skills/uplift-path-design/design-export/sitemap.md`,
which specifies the eight homepage sections and their intended content.

## 1. Broken

Ordered by how badly it hits a real visitor.

| # | Route / component | What a visitor experiences |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/` — every section, `navbar-05.jsx`, `footer-09.jsx` | **Not one link on the page works.** All 53 `<a>` elements are `href="#"`. Nav, mega-menu, hero cards, footer columns, social icons, legal links — everything. The site has 18 real routes under `app/` and the homepage links to none of them. A visitor cannot leave the homepage. |
| 2 | Navbar — `components/sections/navbar-05.jsx` | **All three dropdown mega-menus open and close as one.** A single `isDropdownOpen` state is shared by the "Uplift Solutions" and both "Resources" wrappers. Hovering or clicking any one of them makes all three panels visible simultaneously, all positioned at `top: 74px, left: 2px, right: 1423px` with `z-index: auto` — three full-width panels (734px, 691px and 407px tall) stacked directly on top of each other. The shortest one paints last, so the visitor sees the "Case Studies / Careers" panel overlaying the bottom 327px of the "Uplift Solutions" panel. Verified: clicking one trigger flips all three from `hidden` to `visible`. |
| 3 | Site-wide, `app/globals.css` line 509–511 | **A 2px solid black frame is drawn around the entire page.** The rule `body { border-width: 2px; }` (carried over verbatim from `design-export/globals.original.css` line 392) resolves to `border: 2px solid rgb(0,0,0)`. Every section is inset to `left: 2px / right: 1423px` inside a 1425px viewport. It reads as a stray black box outline around the whole site. |
| 4 | Navbar — `navbar-05.jsx`, desktop CTA | The **"Contact" button in the top-right is dead.** It is a bare `<button>` with no `href`, no `onClick` and no handler. It is the only call-to-action above the fold and it does nothing. |
| 5 | `comparison-09.jsx` | The **"Get Started" button below the comparison table is dead** — a bare `<button>` with no handler. It is the only CTA in that section. |
| 6 | `cta-40.jsx` — main page CTA | **The email signup form is unwired and leaks the address into the URL.** The `<form>` has no `action`, no `method` and no `onSubmit`. Submitting performs a native GET to the same page, so the visitor's email address ends up in the browser address bar and history as `?email=…`, and nothing is ever sent anywhere. This is the primary conversion point on the homepage. |
| 7 | `footer-09.jsx` | **The footer "Subscribe" form goes nowhere.** `handleSubmit` calls `preventDefault()` and then `console.log({ email })`. The visitor gets no confirmation and no error; the address is discarded. |
| 8 | `cta-40.jsx` — email input | **The email field is effectively invisible.** Its only visual affordance is a bottom border computed at `color(srgb 1 1 1 / 0.1)` — white at 10% opacity — sitting on the `rgb(44, 62, 80)` navy section fill. There is no fill and no other border. Visitors will not see there is a field to type in. |
| 9 | `cta-40.jsx` + `footer-09.jsx` | **Duplicate DOM id.** Both email inputs use `id="email"`, so the document contains two elements with the same id. Invalid HTML; breaks label/`for` association and any analytics or autofill keyed on the id. |
| 10 | Navbar — `navbar-05.jsx` | **Two different nav items are both labelled "Resources".** The second nav item ("Overview / Consultation / AI / Advisory / Systems / Compliance" + "From the blog") and the fourth ("Case Studies / FAQs / Careers") carry identical labels. The second one is almost certainly mislabelled — its contents are services, not resources. |

**Clean:** no console errors, no failed network requests, no broken images (every `naturalWidth > 0`),
no horizontal overflow at either breakpoint, and the mobile menu opens and closes correctly.
Fonts load and resolve correctly (Playfair Display for headings, Lexend Deca for body, all `woff2` 200 OK).
**No white-on-teal contrast violation exists on this page** — all buttons use dark text `rgb(0,10,8)`
on `rgb(8,209,167)` at **10.21:1**, which is the approved pairing. The white card text in the hero
and Who-We-Help sections sits over a verified 50% near-black scrim, which is the approved treatment.

## 2. Fake or placeholder content

Grouped by section, top of page first.

### Hero — `components/sections/home/layout-422.jsx` (first thing anyone sees)

1. **The two hero cards are byte-for-byte identical.** Both show eyebrow "For businesses" and heading
   "Build a foundation that holds steady under pressure". Only one of the two was ever written.
2. **The section eyebrow contradicts both cards.** The section is introduced as "For individuals",
   then both cards underneath say "For businesses". The individuals half of the business is missing
   from the hero entirely.
3. **Lorem ipsum is visible on desktop hover, twice.** Each card's hover-reveal contains
   "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit. Suspendisse varius enim in eros
   elementum tristique." A visitor hovering either hero card sees Latin filler.
4. **Two buttons literally labelled "Button"** — the hover-reveal CTA on each card.
5. **Mobile and desktop show different copy for the same card.** Mobile shows the real line
   ("You carry weight that deserves a steady hand" + "Explore"); desktop hover shows the lorem ipsum
   + "Button". The real copy was written but never carried into the desktop variant.
6. **Both hero images still carry Relume's alt text** — `alt="Relume placeholder image 1"` and
   `alt="Relume placeholder image 2"` on `/images/home-hero-section-0.jpg` and `-1.jpg`.

### Who We Help — `components/sections/home/layout-423.jsx`

7. **All three cards are identical** — every one reads "Operational consulting" / "Discovery & Listening".
8. **Four of the five specified services are missing.** `sitemap.md` specifies this section as
   "How We Help Organizations Thrive" with cards for Operational Consulting, Process Optimization,
   Leadership & Strategy, Organizational Systems, and Workflow & Communication Support. Only
   "Operational Consulting" exists, repeated three times.
9. **Lorem ipsum in all three hover-reveals** (same Latin string as the hero).
10. **Three more buttons labelled "Button"**.
11. **All three images carry `alt="Relume placeholder image 1/2/3"`.**

### Comparison table — `components/sections/home/comparison-09.jsx`

12. **Both column headers are Relume's own logo.** Two `<img src="https://d22po4pjz3o32e.cloudfront.net/relume-logo.svg">`
    with `alt="Relume icon 1"` and `alt="Relume icon 2"`. This is the design tool's own brand mark
    presented as if it identified the two things being compared.
13. **The columns are therefore unlabelled.** With both headers showing the same logo, nothing tells
    the visitor which column is Uplift Path and which is "going it alone".
14. **On mobile the only text label disappears.** The "Our approach" heading is `hidden md:flex`, so
    at 375px it computes to `display: none` and the table is two anonymous logo-headed columns.
15. **The competitor column has checkmarks.** Rows "Operational systems that scale" and "Measurable
    milestones and tracking" render a `Check` icon in *both* columns — the section headline is
    "The difference is clear", and two of five rows show no difference at all.
16. **Row 1 breaks the pattern**, using the words "Yes" and "No" while the other four rows use
    check/cross icons.

### Testimonial — `components/sections/home/testimonial-10.jsx`

17. **The same testimonial appears twice.** Both carousel slides carry the identical quote
    ("The fog lifted…"), the identical name **Sarah Mitchell**, and the identical title
    "Executive Director, Behavioral Health". The carousel has two slides and one testimonial.
18. **The avatar is Relume's grey placeholder**, twice —
    `https://d22po4pjz3o32e.cloudfront.net/placeholder-image.svg`, `alt="Testimonial avatar 1"`
    (note the alt is "1" on *both* slides).
19. **The client logo next to the testimonial is a Webflow logo**, twice — `alt="Webflow logo 1"`.
    It is presented as the testimonial-giver's organisation.

### FAQ — `components/sections/home/faq-01.jsx`

20. **Generic consulting boilerplate.** "What is business consulting?" and "Why should we work with a
    business consultant?" are textbook-definition answers that describe the consulting industry, not
    Uplift Path. Nothing names a service, a location, a price, a timeline or a process.
21. **Nothing about mental health.** All four questions are business-consulting only, on a homepage
    whose own hero promises "compassionate mental-health support".
22. **Section header is filler** — "Find answers to your questions about us."
23. **Doesn't match the spec.** `sitemap.md` calls for a steps section titled "Our Approach" with
    four steps: Discover, Assess, Strategize, Support Growth. Worth confirming which is intended.

### Main CTA — `components/sections/home/cta-40.jsx`

24. **This is the wrong section relative to the spec.** `sitemap.md` specifies a dark navy CTA banner
    headlined "Let's Build More Effective Systems Together" with a "Schedule a Consultation" button.
    What ships is a newsletter signup headlined "Ready to unlock Your growth plan" with a "Sign up" button.
25. **The copy and the control contradict each other.** The paragraph says "Book your discovery call
    for personalized, actionable strategies" — the form collects an email and the button says "Sign up".
26. **The image is a Relume placeholder** — `alt="Relume placeholder image"`, served from an expiring
    `imagedelivery.net` URL (see section 5).
27. **"Terms and Conditions" is not a link.** The consent line names them but there is nothing to click,
    even though `/terms-of-use` exists.
28. **Headline has a stray mid-sentence capital and no punctuation** — "Ready to unlock Your growth plan".

### Navbar — `components/sections/navbar-05.jsx`

29. **The "From the blog" panel is literary filler.** Four fake posts: "The fight / Man is not made for
    defeat", "True north / A clean and well-lighted place", "The stream / Big two-hearted river",
    "The code / Grace under pressure" — Hemingway allusions shipped as Relume's demo content.
30. **Seven grey placeholder images in the mega-menus** —
    `https://d22po4pjz3o32e.cloudfront.net/placeholder-image-landscape.svg`, all with
    `alt="Relume placeholder image 1"` or `"2"`.
31. **The three real mega-menu photos are borrowed from another page.** They are
    `/images/terms-of-use-navbar-0.jpg`, `-1.jpg` and `-2.jpg` — Terms-of-Use assets used to illustrate
    Behavioral Health Consulting, Startups & Entrepreneurs and Nonprofit Organizations. All three still
    carry `alt="Relume placeholder image 1/2"`.
32. **Relume's own icon is used as a menu glyph, twice** — `<RelumeIcon />` is the icon for the
    "Advisory" and "Systems" entries, while every neighbouring entry uses a Material Symbol.
33. **Two typos in menu labels** — "Business **Consulatation**" and "**Ai** Consultation".
34. **Three mismatched or generic menu descriptions:** the "FAQs" entry is described as "Virtual
    counseling for emotional well-being."; the "For Individuals" case-study entry reuses the advisory
    blurb ("Strategic guidance tailored for behavioral health, nonprofit, education…"); and the
    "Overview" entry reads "Solutions for your enterprise".
35. **The mobile menu's two CTAs are both labelled "Button".** They are the only CTAs in the mobile
    menu — the "Contact" button is `hidden lg:flex`, so mobile visitors get no Contact CTA at all.

### Footer — `components/sections/footer-09.jsx`

36. **"About us" and "Quick links" are near-duplicate columns.** "About us" contains Home / Services /
    Industries / Contact — none of which is about-us content — and "Quick links" repeats all four plus
    "About". Nine links, five destinations, all dead.
37. **The footer blurb is business-only** — "Unlock growth with expert advice and clear strategies
    tailored to your business goals" — on a site that also sells mental-health services.
38. **Social links are unconnected and the naming is stale** — Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, Linkedin
    all `href="#"`; the icon component is `XLogo` while the label still says "Twitter".

## 3. Design / alignment mistakes

| # | Where | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every button on the page | **No focus indicator whatsoever.** `components/ui/button.jsx` applies `focus-visible:outline-none` and nothing replaces it. Measured on "Contact", "Get Started", "Sign up" and "Subscribe": `outline-style: none`, `box-shadow: none`, and no colour change between rest and focus. Keyboard visitors cannot tell where they are. (Hover *is* fine — `hover:bg-caribbean-green-dark` resolves to `#06a785` correctly.) |
| 2 | Whole page | **Heading hierarchy is inverted.** The page has no `<h1>` above the fold — the hero uses `<h2>` ("Carry less alone"). The only `<h1>` is "The difference is clear", buried in the third section. Eight mega-menu `<h4>`s precede every other heading in the DOM. Order as rendered: H4×8 → H2 → H3 → H2 → H3 → **H1** → H5 → H2 → H3 → H2 → H2×3. |
| 3 | `layout-423.jsx` images | **Three low-resolution images stretched across full-bleed cards.** `home-who-we-help-1.jpg` is natively 291×437 (18 KB) but renders at 405×630 — a 1.4× upscale at 1×, 2.8× on a retina display. `-2.jpg` is 384×576 (25 KB) and `-0.jpg` is 444×666 (30 KB), all rendered at 405×630. For contrast the two hero images in the section above are 2560×1706 at 550–625 KB. The Who-We-Help band will look conspicuously soft next to the hero. |
| 4 | `testimonial-10.jsx` | **The logo beside the testimonial is squeezed to a sliver.** The source is portrait (582×962) but constrained by `max-h-12`, so it renders **29×48** — a 29px-wide strip next to a 56px avatar. |
| 5 | Navbar mega-menus | **Thirteen icons have no `alt` attribute at all** (not even `alt=""`). Every Material Symbols `<img>` in both mega-menus omits it, so screen readers announce the filenames. |
| 6 | `footer-09.jsx` | **Footer column headings violate the type rule.** "About us", "Quick links" and "Connect" are `<h2>` rendering in Playfair Display at **weight 600**. The design guide states headings are Playfair Display weight 400, never bold. Every other heading on the page correctly computes to 400. |
| 7 | Section colour schemes | **Scheme assignments drift from the sitemap.** Hero renders `scheme-1` (spec: 4), Who We Help `scheme-2` (spec: 5), CTA `scheme-3` (spec: 2). This is the known `DESIGN.md`-vs-`globals.css` scheme conflict flagged in `production.md`; the page still looks coherent, but it is worth reconciling deliberately so section colours stop drifting from the design source. |

**Checked and clean:** section rhythm is consistent (every section `padding: 112px` top and bottom at
desktop, `64px` at mobile; footer `80px`/`48px` by design). Both form rows are correctly aligned —
input and button share identical top and bottom edges with a 16px gap at both breakpoints. No
overlapping, clipped or overflowing content at either breakpoint; `scrollWidth` equals `clientWidth`
at 375px and 1440px. The `col-span-3` cells inside the mobile 2-column comparison grid create a
harmless 0px third track and do not misalign anything.

## 4. Reused-content signal

Everything below repeats *within the homepage alone*, which is the clearest evidence it was never
authored for this business. (A cross-page sweep would likely find more — the testimonial and the
CTA section in particular are shared components used on other routes.)

| Asset / content | Times on `/` | Where |
|---|---|---|
| `placeholder-image-landscape.svg` (Relume grey placeholder) | 7 | Navbar mega-menus — "Industries we Support" ×1, "From the blog" ×4, "Careers" ×2 |
| "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet…" paragraph | 5 | `layout-422` ×2, `layout-423` ×3 (hover reveals) |
| Button labelled literally "Button" | 6 | `layout-422` ×2, `layout-423` ×3, mobile menu ×2 (one overlaps) |
| "Operational consulting / Discovery & Listening" card | 3 | `layout-423` — all three cards |

| `placeholder-image.svg` testimonial avatar | 2 | `testimonial-10` (both tagged `alt="Testimonial avatar 1"`) |
| Webflow logo (`imagedelivery.net`) | 2 | `testimonial-10` (both tagged `alt="Webflow logo 1"`) |
| `relume-logo.svg` | 2 | `comparison-09` — both column headers |
| Nav label "Resources" | 2 | Navbar — two different menus, same label |
| Footer links Home / Services / Contact / Industries | 2 each | "About us" and "Quick links" columns |

### CTA wording inconsistency

Six different labels are used for what are effectively two actions (get in touch / subscribe), and
none matches the "Schedule a Consultation" wording the sitemap specifies:

| Label | Where | Destination |
|---|---|---|
| "Contact" | Navbar (desktop only) | nothing |
| "Get Started" | `comparison-09` | nothing |
| "Explore" | Hero cards (mobile only) | `#` |
| "Button" | Hero + Who We Help hover, mobile menu | `#` / nothing |
| "Sign up" | `cta-40` | unwired form |
| "Subscribe" | `footer-09` | `console.log` |

### Navbar / footer chrome

Both are mounted once in `app/layout.tsx` and are therefore identical on every route — no drift to
report. The issues in them (dead links, shared dropdown state, duplicate "Resources" label,
placeholder blog entries) affect all 19 routes, not just the homepage.

## 5. Expiring assets

> **That timestamp is 2026-09-02T00:00:00Z — seven days from today.** After it passes these images
> return 403 and render as broken. Note this is *two days earlier* than the "4 Sep 2026" stated in
> `.claude/skills/uplift-path-design/production.md`; the signature itself is the authority.
