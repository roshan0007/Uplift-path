Wraps a video embed at a fixed 16:9 ratio, showing a spinning `progress_activity` glyph until the iframe loads.

```jsx
<VideoEmbed src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/dQw4w9WgXcQ" />
```

On the source site it is always inside a `Dialog`, so the loader is white. Widths there are 738px at md and 940px at lg.
